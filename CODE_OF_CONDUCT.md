# Community code of conduct

We welcome participants of all backgrounds and experience levels. Discuss ideas and evidence respectfully. Give actionable feedback, acknowledge uncertainty, and respect other people's time and privacy.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Do not post credentials or another person's private information.

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests and other project-managed spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and warn or restrict participants, with an explanation where safe and appropriate. Enforcement should be proportionate and consistent. Disagreement with a technical decision is not misconduct.

For a sensitive report, request a private contact from @jkcary without publishing personal details. Reports involving the lead maintainer currently have no independent appeal body; participants may ask for a mutually agreed independent reviewer. Do not imply confidentiality guarantees on public GitHub threads.

欢迎不同背景的参与者。请就事论事、尊重隐私，提供具体可执行的反馈。禁止骚扰、歧视、人身攻击和公开他人隐私。维护者会按情况采取提醒、移除内容或限制参与等措施，并尽可能说明理由。
